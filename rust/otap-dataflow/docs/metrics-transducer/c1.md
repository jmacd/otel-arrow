# C1. Resolve intervals before dropping writer identity

[Worklist](../metrics-transducer-worklist.md)

`Resolve` belongs between `Select` and `Window` in the proposed transducer,
not in the exporter. It consumes [pdata metric views][views] keyed by
resource/entity, scope, descriptor, point attributes and logical writer.
Borrow the hierarchy of the [stream identity code][identity], but compare
full typed keys on hash matches; never let discarded attributes or batch row
IDs identify a predecessor. Only resolved contributions may lose dimensions.

## What the first point establishes

A source epoch is one writer's unbroken measurement sequence. For a valued
OTel sum or ordinary histogram, distinguish three cases:

- **Delta:** take the entire value on its reported `(s,t]`, with `s < t`.
  The first regional delta of 7 counts as 7; it needs no prior observation.
  Reject missing or reversed coverage and conflicting overlaps. Suppress a
  retry by its durable input/publication identity, not by numeric equality.
- **Known cumulative start:** a genuine start `s < t` declares a zero at
  `s`. With no predecessor in that epoch the first candidate is the entire
  value on `(s,t]`, subject to pane admissibility below. Later observations
  with that start contribute differences, not repeated lifetime totals.
- **Unknown cumulative start:** an explicit `start == end` is a valued
  baseline, not a zero or an increment. For an absent start (zero in the
  [OTAP point view][otap]), establish the same baseline at the series' first
  event time `T`. Keep `(T,T,900)`; the next value 907 at `U` has normalized
  cumulative form `(T,U,907)` and contributes 7 on `(T,U]`. Reuse `T` for
  subsequent missing starts, including after evaluator recovery.

A StatsD counter increment is an observation at an instant rather than a
pre-aggregated OTel interval. [E1](e1.md) preserves its measurement kind and
ingress time, so `requests:3:4|c` adds 7 to the pane containing that time.
Do not treat either increment as a zero-duration cumulative baseline or
guess the coverage of an ordinary OTel delta with a missing start.

A writer restart with a new nonoverlapping start or an unknown-start marker
opens a new epoch. If 907 is followed by 2 from a new known zero, consider
the 2 in full, never `2 - 907`. Reject overlapping purported writer epochs.
A decrease in an explicitly unchanged monotonic epoch is inconsistent:
report it, not a rollover. With missing starts, a monotonic decrease signals
a discontinuity; record the lower observation as a new unknown baseline.
A signed sum decrease alone is not evidence of restart. An evaluator restart
must restore or replay the predecessor; it is not evidence of a source reset.

## Which differences enter a pane

Keep observations ordered by event time within each epoch. For adjacent `p,t`
subtract values, or subtract count, aligned bucket counts (including zero
count) and available sum for histograms under [C3](c3.md). Reject negative
count differences and conflicting observations at one endpoint. A2 admits
only whole intervals contained in a required right-closed pane. With minute
panes, `(58,62]` straddles 60: reject and report that contribution, whether
supplied or derived. Do not assign it by end time or prorate it. A valid
cumulative observation at 62 remains a baseline for `(62,70]`.

Retain the predecessor at or before the open edge. Inserting a late point
between 900 and 907 with value 903 replaces the old edge of 7 with edges of
3 and 4; recompute affected open cells, never add all three. An observation
at 60 could likewise resolve the straddler while both panes remain open.
Count arrivals requiring a sealed change as late; never move a committed
baseline or modify a sealed result on replay.
