## Rank conditioning versus a known weight total

Use the G1 fixture: A, B, C contribute `(+8,-2,+1)`; `k = 2`. The exact
answer for `host=B` is -2, for all hosts +7; the total weight is 11.
Under the pinned draws the rank-conditioned (RC) subset estimate is
`-8/3`. To check unbiasedness without a stochastic tolerance, fix A and C
as in G1 and enumerate B's two uniform-draw regions. For `U_B <= 1/4`
the estimate is zero (ignore the probability-zero tie); for `U_B > 1/4`
it is `-8/3`. Their probability-weighted sum is -2. Splitting A's +8
into eight accepted +1 records must not give A eight chances to win.

When exact `W` is available for this **same** weighted population, compare
Cohen and Kaplan's [subset conditioning][paper] (SC) for exponential
ranks. SC conditions on the *set* of the first `k-1` other items, not
their cutoff rank. Here is a small enumerable oracle. For winning set `S`,
let `L = W - sum(w in S)`. Define `h(A)` as the probability that all items
in `A` arrive in an exponential race before the first outsider of rate `L`.
With the same `L` throughout, enumerate first arrivals by weight:

```text
h(empty) = 1
h(A) = sum over j in A of w_j / (L + sum(w in A)) * h(A without j)
p_SC(i) = h(S) / h(S without i)
estimate(P) = sum over matching winners i of v_i / p_SC(i)
```

In the pinned sample `S={A,B}`, `L=1` and
`h(S) = (8/11)(2/3) + (2/11)(8/9) = 64/99`. Thus B's SC estimate is
`-2 * (8/9) / (64/99) = -11/4`, not RC's `-8/3`. Enumerating the other
sets, `{B,C}` has probability `19/495` and gives `-110/19`; `{A,C}`
contributes zero. The weighted mean is exactly -2. For **all** hosts on the
pinned draw, RC gives `456/85`, SC gives `11/2`, and the committed exact
answer is +7. SC's adjusted **magnitudes** sum to 11 in each sample.
Neither estimator promises smaller error on every draw. Do not feed SC
the signed metric total, infer `W` from winners, or normalize RC to +7.

For skew, repeat with `(+100,-2,+1)` and enumerate the same races. In
keep-all both estimators equal every exact subset sum; for an empty or
all-zero population both return zero. RC is the baseline when `W` is
absent. SC is a comparison, not a required production default; the
exponential-cost enumeration above is for small fixtures, not a hot path.

## Five minutes are not a union of one-minute series sketches

Use five sealed minutes, `k = 1`, same service, and pin `U = 1/2` for
every series to make the arithmetic checkable. In minute `j = 1..5`, host
H contributes +4; a one-minute host `A_j` contributes `9+j` and a distinct
one-minute host `B_j` contributes `4+j`. In units of `ln(2)`, ranks are
the reciprocal weights. Every minute A wins, B is first excluded and H
is absent even from runner-up state. Across five minutes H has weight 20,
larger than any competitor (maximum 14). **H is the direct winner.** The
exact service delta is `20 + 60 + 35 = 115` in either evaluation. No merge
of these five winner lists can recover H's identity or rank.

Choose and label one of these bounded downstream contracts:

- For an **interval estimate**, keep at most a configured number of
  sealed pane summaries and sum their estimates for the same predicate
  over whole, nonoverlapping accepted increments. In this draw H's pane
  estimates sum to 0 versus the exact +20. Linearity gives unbiasedness
  under the G1 model without independent panes; reused draws correlate
  errors. Do not report independent-error bars or call it a parent sketch.
  To estimate cumulative change, add interval estimates since the output
  epoch's baseline; for a level also require its known starting value.
  Never difference unrelated sampled cumulative series.
- For a **direct parent sketch**, retain or send all original per-series
  interval partials within a capped horizon and byte budget, or replay
  pinned input. Aggregate by original key at five minutes, then rank once.
  Disjoint owners may instead send compatible target-interval sketches
  with unchanged weights for [G3's spatial merge](g3.md). Require +20
  for H's original value, H as winner and `tau = ln(2)/14` here.
- If coverage, predicate fields or rebuild state have expired, or a request
  exceeds its fan-in/byte cap, report the estimate unavailable or explicitly
  incomplete. Do not silently drop panes or substitute the exact service
  total for the missing subset. The supported horizon is operator policy.

## Acceptance at the pipeline boundary

Compare direct, staged and replayed results on *disjoint* edge series
with identical weights: G1 winners, values and first-excluded rank must
match. For repeated hosts use the contract above, not winner equality.
In the three-host fixture, accept a late +2 from B before seal: its net
change becomes zero, so A and C now win with probability one. Replay must
restore this replacement; under the sealed-only policy the same late input
after seal must not change it. Check reset/unknown-start deltas and no-data
markers against the exact per-host oracle before sampling.

For G2 split the [tied trace fixture](g2.md) across edges and minutes;
require the same IDs, cutoff and representative as direct selection.
Duplicate a trace with an earlier measurement, retry a publication and
crash on both sides of commit. No extra slot, reroll or duplicate delta
is allowed. Exercise keep-all, no links and fixed thresholds separately.
Exhaust each count and byte budget with the other mode enabled: inspect
both sizes/statuses and compare the exact metric to a both-disabled run.
